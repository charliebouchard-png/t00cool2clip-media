# GTA 6 clipping rules (from gtaclipper.com/guides, read 2026-11-21)

Apply to every GTA 6 / streamer clip, campaign or client.

## Making the clip
- Layout: stream clips use `"layout":"stream"` in the plan (facecam on top 1080x760, gameplay below). The streamer must be the main focus.
- 15-40 s, one moment. Open on the action (no run-up, menus or "wait for it"); hook card in the first 2 s, 3-5 words, true (never "leak").
- Hook -> setup -> payoff -> stop one beat after the payoff. Must make sense with the sound off (captions on).
- Game sound + voice only. No trending songs on paid clips (TikTok Commercial Music Library only).
- Never: story spoilers, cutscene compilations, leaks, cheats/exploits, in-game TV shows on their own, other clippers' clips.

## Posting a paid (campaign) clip
- Caption and YouTube title START with `#ad` (post_item.sh adds it for items with a `campaign` field), plus the platform paid label
  (TikTok "Disclose commercial content > Branded content"; YouTube "Paid promotion"). Missing disclosure = rejection.
- YouTube title: "GTA 6" + the moment in the first words (people search Shorts). Required tags first, then 2-3 own.
- Submit within 30 min. Never edit or delete a post after submitting.
- Earnings: approved clips earn for 7 days, up to a per-clip cap. Many clips that reach the cap beat one viral clip.
- Prefer CPM campaigns with low minimums (e.g. $1-2/1K) and logo campaigns (any GTA 6 streamer) over pool campaigns
  (pools need 750K+ total approved views before paying anything).

## Selling clips (clients)
- Targets: GTA 6 streamers/YouTubers 20K-1M subs with a business email, FiveM/GTA RP servers.
- Offer 2 free sample clips, then GBP 5/clip (bulk 10+ from GBP 4). Leads live on the Outreach Tracker ("GTA 6 creator - clip editing").
